# Reporting

## What is it?

Reporting is the final and most critical phase of a penetration test. It involves documenting all findings, writing executive and technical summaries, and providing actionable remediation recommendations. A well-crafted report communicates risk to both technical teams and business leadership.

## Why Learn It?

A penetration test without a proper report delivers no value. Reporting translates technical findings into business risk, enabling stakeholders to prioritize remediation. Professional reports establish credibility, meet compliance requirements, and serve as reference documents for future assessments.

## You Will Learn

- Report structure: executive summary, methodology, findings, recommendations
- Writing clear and concise technical findings with CVSS scoring
- Creating executive summaries for non-technical audiences
- Remediation recommendations and prioritization strategies
- Legal documentation and proof of testing scope
