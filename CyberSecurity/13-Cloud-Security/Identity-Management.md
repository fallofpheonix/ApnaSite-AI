# Identity Management

## What is it?

Cloud identity and access management (IAM) controls who can access what resources and what actions they can perform. It encompasses federation, single sign-on (SSO), multi-factor authentication (MFA), and role-based access control (RBAC). Proper IAM is the first line of defense against unauthorized access in cloud environments.

## Why Learn It?

Identity is the new perimeter in cloud computing. With no fixed network boundary, controlling and verifying identity becomes critical. Weak IAM policies—overly broad roles, missing MFA, or poor federation—have led to some of the largest cloud breaches in history.

## You Will Learn

- Cloud IAM models across AWS, Azure, and GCP
- Federated identity and SSO protocols like SAML and OIDC
- MFA implementation strategies and best practices
- Role-based access control and least-privilege principles
- Identity governance and lifecycle management
