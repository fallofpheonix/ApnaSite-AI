# ADR-003: AI Produces Proposals, Never State Mutations

> **Status:** Accepted
> **Date:** 2026-07-13
> **Supersedes:** None
> **Superseded By:** None
> **Affects:** AI Proposal Context, Domain Model

## Context

The AI layer needs to suggest changes to business data. If AI directly mutated state, there would be no review step, no audit trail, and no way to correct AI mistakes.

## Decision

The AI layer produces `Proposal` entities containing `SuggestedChange` objects. Proposals are never applied directly. They are reviewed and accepted by the owner before becoming Commands.

## Consequences

**Positive:**
- Owner maintains control over all changes
- Full audit trail of AI suggestions
- Easy to correct AI mistakes
- AI confidence and reasoning are captured

**Negative:**
- Extra step in the workflow
- Owner must actively review proposals
